THE ARNINES    

R1,R4,R6,R7,R9

About

The Arnines are subway cars built for the IND 8 avenue line, the R1 types entering service alongside the 8 av line on september 10 1932, the R1 cars were numbered 100 to 399. The R4 was introduced due to the INDs expansion, with cars numbered 400 to 899.    

The R6 was consisted of 500 more new cars, but this order was a lot more complex, The R6-3 was numbered 900 to 1149, the R6-2 Was numbered 1150 to 1299, the R6-1 was numbered 1300 to 1399. the difference between the R1 and R4s and the R6 is the R6 having a split pane on the front door.   

The R7 Was also split into two contracts, the R7 and R7A, the R7 was numbered 1400 to 1549, and the R7A was numbered 1550 to 1649. Then there was the R9, numbered 1650 to 1802, 1800 to 1802 were built as replacements to R1 cars 212 and 378 and R4 car 472, which were involved in a wreck in 1936. all Arnines have the same specifcations aswell.

More info on specific cars.

R4 cars 484 and 744 were fitted with bullseye lighting. in 1946 R7A car 1575 was involved in a wreck, and was rebuilt as a prototype for the R10 cars. in 1960 R1 car 273 was involved in a wreck and scrapped. in 1971 cars 165 and 192 were cut in half and extended to 75 feet to test curves and tunnels in the subway and to see if the R44 and R46 cars would be possible. in 1971 car 1761 was involved in a runaway train at east new york yard. in the 1970s to 1980s some arnines were painted in the silver and blue livery but most retained their original paint job, then the retirement came. All arnines were retired on the 31 march 1977 on the J line.      

 

 

Preservation

R4 car 825 is preserved by the trolley museum of new york and kingston, R6 car 923 and 925 are preserved and are awaiting restoration as of now, parts of R6 car 978 were inside a dehli but closed in the 2010s and is preserved elsewere, R6 983 was used as a tool shed in jcksinville california and was taken and is preserved at the craggy mountainline, R6 car 1144 is preserved by the buckinghamshire railway center in england, i have seen it myself and the photo abouve is mine,  and is the only nyc subway car outside america. and thats all the preserved cars.
